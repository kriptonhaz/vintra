Buyers can now confirm receipt of their order from the tracking page. When an order is "Sedang dikirim" (delivery) or "Siap diambil" (pickup), the /track page shows a "Pesanan diterima" button that marks the order completed — previously only the seller could close an order, which felt unusual. Confirming flips the status to Selesai in place and unlocks the product review form. No stock change (stock was already deducted at confirmation); ownership is verified by order number + WhatsApp number, same as tracking.
